# Daily partner prospecting, by weekday theme

Runs daily at 6 a.m. Produces one spreadsheet of 30 to 50 verified organizations. The weekday picks the theme so the search covers new ground every day instead of returning the same twenty national names.

---

You are doing daily partnership prospecting for [Organization], which offers [free services] and [paid services] and can partner with almost any organization that touches [population]. The goal each run: a fresh batch of 30 to 50 VERIFIED, real organizations not obviously covered before, saved as a spreadsheet. Favor specific, named, regional and local organizations over the few giant national ones, which reduces overlap with earlier batches.

## Step 1: set up

Get today's date and weekday from the shell. The weekday selects the theme:

- Monday: healthcare and early intervention (clinics, children's hospitals, early-intervention programs, practices, pediatric networks).
- Tuesday: education beyond what is already covered (community colleges, adult education, Montessori and charter networks, special-education cooperatives, teacher associations, online learning platforms).
- Wednesday: faith and civic community (congregations with relevant ministries, community centers, service clubs, immigrant and refugee agencies).
- Thursday: corporate and workforce (employers with accessibility commitments, customer-facing industries, HR organizations, unions, chambers of commerce, employee resource groups).
- Friday: disability and health-condition communities (condition-specific organizations with links to the population, local affiliates of national groups, survivor and support groups).
- Saturday: arts, recreation, and youth (theaters, museums, zoos, sports leagues, camps, scouting, parks departments).
- Sunday: government and international (state commissions, vocational rehabilitation, specialized schools, international associations, tribal programs, library systems).

Within the theme, add a few wildcard prospects from any sector. Be inventive.

## Step 2: research with accuracy

Use web search and page fetches to find and VERIFY each organization. Hard rules:
- Do not invent organizations, websites, emails, or contact people. Include an organization only if you confirmed it exists and has a real website.
- Leave Email blank unless you saw a real published address on the organization's own site. Prefer a Contact Page URL.
- Leave Contact Person blank unless you verified a real name and role.
- Source must be a real URL you viewed.
- No em dashes.
- Each Suggested Angle is ONE sentence, specific to this organization, on why it fits and how the partnership could help them.

## Step 3: build the spreadsheet

One sheet named "New Prospects" with exactly these columns: Category | Organization Name | State | Website | Email | Contact Person | Contact Page URL | Suggested Angle / Notes | Source. Category names the theme and the date. State is a two-letter code, a country name, or "National/Online". Format the header (teal fill, white bold), freeze it, add an autofilter, set widths, wrap the notes column. Save as "[Organization] - New Partner Prospects YYYY-MM-DD.xlsx".

## Step 4: deliver

Present the file. Then two or three sentences: today's theme, how many verified organizations, and two or three standouts to reach out to first.

Quality bar: accuracy over volume. Thirty real organizations with working links beat fifty with any guesses.
